**Title:** Permission request: cdn.islamic.network ayah audio in a free, non-commercial student app (MyQuran)

Assalamu'alaikum,

JazakAllahu khairan for alquran.cloud and the Islamic Network CDN.

I am Zainul Arkaan, a student from Indonesia. I build **MyQuran**, a free Android app for
reading, learning and memorizing the Qur'an. It is a personal student project that is also
part of my school coursework, and it is completely non-commercial: no ads, no in-app
purchases, no sale of user data. It is distributed on GitHub and is being submitted to
Google Play.

**How the app uses your service:**

- It streams per-ayah recitations from `cdn.islamic.network` when a user plays a verse
  (reciter chosen by the user, bitrate from your metadata).
- Users can also download a surah's ayah audio to their own phone for offline listening.
  Files stay on that device only. We never re-host, bundle or redistribute your audio.
- The app caches nothing on our own servers; each request goes straight to your CDN.

**Terms I commit to** (happy to follow any conditions you set):

1. **Attribution:** "Audio: Islamic Network CDN (alquran.cloud)" plus the reciter's name, on
   the app's "Sources & licences" page and in the audio player's reciter list.
2. **No modification:** recordings are played as delivered, never edited or mixed.
3. **No redistribution:** no re-hosting and no bundling of audio in the APK.
4. **Non-commercial:** the app stays free; any change would need new permission first.
5. **Fair use of the CDN:** requests happen only on user action. No bulk scraping, no prefetching.
6. **Withdrawal:** if you ask me to stop, the source is removed within 14 days.
7. **No endorsement:** no claim that Islamic Network endorses the app.

**Could you confirm this use is okay, and tell me the attribution wording you prefer?**
