I kindly ask for your permission to use the **page and line layout data** of the Madinah

page and line, so the app can arrange the Qur'an text the way the printed mushaf does.

- The Qur'an text itself comes from Tanzil, unchanged.
- We obtained the layout data through QUL (Tarteel) and have sent them a similar request.
- The mushaf page view is **disabled** in the published app (it shows "coming soon") until
  we receive your permission.

**Terms we commit to** (we will follow any conditions you set):

- **Article 2 — No changes:** the order of words, lines and pages is not altered.

  the app's "Sources & licences" page.
- **Article 4 — Non-commercial:** the app stays free; any change would require new permission first.
- **Article 5 — Corrections:** any correction you send is applied within 14 days.
- **Article 6 — Withdrawal:** if permission is withdrawn, the data is removed within 14 days.
- **Article 7 — No endorsement:** we never claim the app is issued or endorsed by the
